Q56: Are there any scholarships or waivers available?
--------------------------------------------------
A56: Yes. Waivers on admission fees are based on HSC GPA.  Merit-based scholarships, waivers for siblings, special waivers for female students (in certain cases), and scholarships for orphans are available from the Shahida Rafique (SR) and Rizia Haque (RH) fund.  Students with a 4.0 GPA in a semester receive a 100% tuition waiver for the next semester. The top two students in each semester's final exam (minimum 3.5 GPA) receive a 50% tuition waiver for the following semester. Poor fund scholarships and teaching assistantships are also available.  An 80% class attendance requirement applies for most waivers/scholarships.
